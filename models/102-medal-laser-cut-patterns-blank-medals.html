Read the geometry before buying

Inspect the preview for narrow bridges, holes, overlapping layers and joints. Decide which features are essential to your intended use, then verify their dimensions with the seller.

Measure the display area and decide whether the piece should read as a bold silhouette or a layered composition. Inspect narrow bridges and mounting options before committing to the file. Read the wall art & décor project guide for the wider planning process.

Your pre-purchase checklist

  • Confirm the file formats and whether an assembly guide is included.
  • Ask which material thickness the joints were designed around.
  • Check the finished dimensions and whether resizing affects the fit.
  • Read the current license for your intended personal or commercial use.
  • Plan a small geometry and fit test before cutting all the parts.

Work through the SVG import checklist and the kerf and slot-fit guide. No machine settings, prices, ratings or performance claims are inferred from this preview.
