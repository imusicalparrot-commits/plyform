Plan mounting and clearance

Measure the intended wall or display area. Check backing layers, hanging hardware and any clearance needed for a photo, mechanism or personalized text before committing to the final scale.

Treat a decorative keepsake as a display object unless its intended use and safety are independently established. Check mounting, small parts and personalized lettering before choosing a file. Read the baby & nursery décor project guide for the wider planning process.

Your pre-purchase checklist

  • Confirm the file formats and whether an assembly guide is included.
  • Ask which material thickness the joints were designed around.
  • Check the finished dimensions and whether resizing affects the fit.
  • Read the current license for your intended personal or commercial use.
  • Plan a small geometry and fit test before cutting all the parts.
