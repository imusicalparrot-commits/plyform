About PLYFORM

An independent editorial project about choosing digital designs and planning laser-cut builds.

What we publish

PLYFORM brings together practical project-selection guides and a reference library of laser-cut design listings. The articles focus on questions a maker can answer before buying: what the object needs to do, which geometry deserves inspection and how material thickness affects assembly.

The library was assembled from the supplied PLYFORM catalog. Titles and product previews identify the referenced designs; the explanatory planning text is written for this site. PLYFORM is the publisher and organizational author of these guides. We do not present a fictional individual author or claim professional credentials.

Our editorial method

A preview can suggest an idea, but it cannot verify a finished size, a material specification or an included file format. Model notes distinguish these unknowns from practical checks. We have not test-cut the referenced designs, so these pages are not hands-on product reviews.

Project categories help readers compare options. The selection is not a ranking of quality, and inclusion is not a certification of safety, compatibility or seller reliability. Consult the original designer for current technical details.
