Privacy policy

Privacy, cookies and external links on PLYFORM.

Data on PLYFORM

PLYFORM is a static website. It has no account registration, message forms, analytics scripts or browser-storage code. The site does not set its own cookies.

The hosting service may process connection information to deliver and secure the website. The interactive home page loads fonts from Google Fonts; external font requests share connection information with that service.

Affiliate links and commissions

Some links to Creative Fabrica and AliExpress are affiliate links. PLYFORM may earn a commission from a qualifying purchase attributed to one of these links. Creative Fabrica links use referral ID 16881082; the materials banner uses the supplied rzekl.com redirect to AliExpress.

Purchases, downloads, licenses and customer support are handled by the destination marketplace. Links retain their sponsored attributes for search engines. No purchase or payment information is collected by this static PLYFORM site.

Cookies

PLYFORM does not set first-party cookies or use local storage. Read the cookie notice for details. External marketplaces, redirect services and the hosting provider may operate under their own cookie and privacy policies.

External websites

Following a marketplace link takes you away from PLYFORM. The destination may use cookies or other tracking under its own policies. Review its current terms before providing personal information, purchasing a file or starting a trial.
