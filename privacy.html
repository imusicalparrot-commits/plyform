Privacy policy

This policy describes the supplied static version of PLYFORM.

Data on this site

This edition contains static pages, locally stored images and a stylesheet. It does not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers, account registration, message forms or browser-storage code. It does not set its own cookies.

The hosting provider may process connection information, such as IP address, browser details and access timestamps, to deliver and secure the website. The provider’s actual retention practices must be confirmed with the deployed hosting service.

External websites and referral tracking

When you choose a labeled affiliate link, your browser visits Creative Fabrica or follows an affiliate redirect to AliExpress, sending the referral information contained in the URL. The marketplace may use cookies or other tracking under its own policies. PLYFORM does not control that processing or receive your payment details from this static website.

Before providing information or completing a purchase, read the marketplace’s current privacy and cookie information. You can browse PLYFORM’s articles without opening affiliate links.

Privacy enquiries

For privacy questions, email joaquin.tate@gmx.com. See the contact page for publisher details. This policy should be updated if the deployed site adds analytics, forms or other data collection.
