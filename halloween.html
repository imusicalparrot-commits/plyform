5 Spooky Laser Cut Ideas for Halloween Decorations

Halloween is the perfect season to bring your creativity to life with laser cut SVG files. From pumpkins and ghosts to themed table decor, here are five spooky ideas that are easy to cut and great for gifting or selling.

1. Layered Pumpkin Ornaments

Multilayer pumpkins create depth and a handcrafted look. Stack cut layers in different sizes to build a 3D pumpkin that stands out on a mantel or shelf.

2. Ghost Table Centerpieces

Soft, rounded ghost shapes make charming centerpieces. Paint them white, add tiny details and arrange them across the table for a playful spooky effect.

3. Haunted House Wall Art

A haunted house panel with layered silhouettes becomes a striking piece of wall decor. It works beautifully as a seasonal statement or a gift for Halloween fans.

4. Trick-or-Treat Sign & Bunting

Custom signs and bunting bring the party together. Cut a welcome sign for the front door and string small flags along the entryway.

5. Personalized Witch or Bat Coasters

Practical and giftable, laser cut witch and bat coasters are fast to produce and perfect for sets or holiday markets.

With a little imagination and clean laser cuts, you can turn a simple sheet of wood into memorable Halloween decor that lasts beyond the season.
