Practical answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose a wall art & décor design?

Measure the display area and decide whether the piece should read as a bold silhouette or a layered composition. Inspect narrow bridges and mounting options before committing to the file.

Can I resize the file freely?

Not always. Scaling changes slots, holes and structural details as well as the outline. Check thickness-dependent geometry and verify fit on a small test piece.

Where are downloads and license details available?

Yes, the files and their license are available through the Creative Fabrica link on each model page. Open the original listing to check its download options.

Is a license included with the download?

Yes. Creative Fabrica downloads include a license. Read the license attached to your download for the permitted personal and commercial uses.

Can I download the design file?

Yes. Open a model page and follow its Creative Fabrica link to find the design files and download options on the original listing.

Can I download designs completely free?

Yes. Creative Fabrica offers free designs and free-trial downloads for eligible users. Availability depends on the design and your account. Trials may renew as paid subscriptions, so check the current terms and cancellation deadline before signing up.
