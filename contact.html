Get in touch

Contact PLYFORM

Questions about a file purchase and questions about our editorial content need different information.

Publisher contact

Email joaquin.tate@gmx.com for questions about PLYFORM editorial content or catalog corrections. You can also find our project collections on the PLYFORM laser-cut Pinterest profile. This site does not collect messages through a form.

Purchases and downloads

PLYFORM does not process purchases or deliver design files. For payment, download, licensing or assembly questions, use the support or designer contact options on your original marketplace purchase. Include the listing name and order reference in your message to that marketplace.

Information needed for a catalog correction

When emailing the publisher, include the PLYFORM model-page URL, the design name and the specific correction. Useful corrections include a removed listing, an incorrect category or a misleading statement about a file. Our editorial approach explains what is and is not verified.
