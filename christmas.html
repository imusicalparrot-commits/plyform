Christmas Lamps — Bring Warm Holiday Magic to Your Home

Light up your festive season with beautifully laser cut Christmas lamps that combine art, craftsmanship and coziness. Whether you love the glow of candlelight or the intricate detail of layered wood designs, these Christmas lamp files are perfect for your next project.

Why Choose a Laser Cut Christmas Lamp Design?

Laser cut Christmas lamps are more than decorations — they are memories made in warm light. With high quality SVG files you can craft your own glowing masterpieces for home decor, Christmas markets or holiday gifts. Each file is optimized for Glowforge, LightBurn and other laser cutting machines, ensuring perfect cuts and easy assembly.

Tips for Crafting Your Perfect Christmas Lamp

  • Use 3–5mm plywood or MDF for sturdy layers and precise engraving
  • Combine warm LED lights with your design for a soft, cozy glow
  • Paint or stain the wood for extra character, or leave it natural for a rustic look
  • Personalize each lamp with family names or short holiday messages

Every Christmas lamp you create tells a story — of love, craftsmanship and festive spirit. Design your own glowing decorations or meaningful gifts that will be cherished year after year.
